It’s been a long time since I’ve seen this one, and it deserves a rewatch from me. But from what I remember, it is an incredible experience. You can FEEL the action, it’s not just screaming in your face. It’s done with thought and taste.

I love the dark atmosphere in this one. It’s brutal and intimate. It’s slow, and it simmers, and the results really melt in your mouth. It really feels like a piece of art.

If you haven’t seen it, then do yourself a favor and give it a watch.
